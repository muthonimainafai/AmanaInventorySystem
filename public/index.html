Retail Feed Inventory

Set price per kg for each product; profit margin per kg is calculated as retail price per kg minus your Feed Inventory buying price per kg (buying price per bag ÷ bag size) when that stock exists. Staff on Sales Per Kg must use these prices when a line exists here; otherwise they use Feed Inventory (selling price ÷ bag size). Accumulated profit on each line increases by margin × kg sold on each sale. Wholesale stock stays under Feed Inventory. For Maize (Germ Wishwa), Broken (Wheat), and Wheat (Bran / Pollard), you can set Weight (kg) per opened bag — staff see it on Sales Per Kg and it drives the Total Kgs column when set.

Chicken Sales Inventory

Record chick sales by breed. Minimum order: 50 chicks. Owner: set buying and selling per chick; profit margin is calculated automatically (selling − buying). Staff: Price per chick follows the selling price last saved by the owner for that breed (from their sales). Choose Feed brand and Feed type from Feed Inventory; Feed amount is bags × that line’s selling price per bag.

Customer Details

Record sale (by kilograms)

Total Kgs is the kg left from opened bags for that product that day (starts at the owner’s weight per bag when set under Retail Feed Inventory, otherwise the catalog bag size, e.g. 50 kg, and goes down as you record sales). Each time you save, Kg sold (this sale) is added onto the same row for that product/day (no duplicate rows). Bags sold counts full bags completed through this line (counts 1 only when accumulated kg in the current bag reaches the full bag size, e.g. 50 kg; a new bag is then opened automatically). Accumulated kg sold is kg sold from the current open bag only (returns to 0 when that bag is finished).
